# Ideas / someday

- Visit no-show: mark a scheduled visit as no-show and optionally track/notify
- **Geolocation / GPS attendance**: use device location and GPS navigation so attendance can be marked automatically when the therapist (or client) is at the site (e.g. within a geofence or radius)
- SMS or email reminders for upcoming appointments
- Patient portal: patients view their visits and invoices
- Multi-location or multi-practice support
- Reporting / export: run reports or export data (e.g. visits, revenue) for a date range
- Invoice reminders: remind staff or patients about unpaid or due invoices
